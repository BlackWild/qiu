## [0.1.0]

### Added

- Gaussian beams through ABCD systems, the phase signals of thin optical elements, the parameters and results of a lens experiment with their storage, a simulation loop for any `PropagationBackend` with an exact backend, the arithmetic of the sample-based phase protocol, classical references, analysis quantities, plots and the command line of the simulations.
